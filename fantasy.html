Know your format

The same player can be a star in one league and bench filler in another. Here's how each common format scores, with tips that follow from the math.

ESPN points

G 2, A 1, PPP 0.5, SHP 0.5, SOG 0.1, HIT 0.1, BLK 0.5. Goalies: W 4, GA -2, SV 0.2, SO 3, OTL 1.

  • One goal equals four blocked shots, or 20 shots, or 20 hits. Shot-blocking defensemen are the cheapest points on waivers.
  • Goalies carry weeks: a 30-save win with 2 against is 6 points, a 30-save shutout win is 13. Chase starts on 4-game teams.
  • A power-play goal is 2.5. A promotion to the top power-play unit shows up right away.

Yahoo points

G 6, A 4, +/- 2, PPP 2, SOG 0.9, BLK 1. Goalies: W 5, GA -3, SV 0.6, SHO 5.

  • Shots are king: 10 shots are 9 points, a goal and a half. High-volume shooters outscore efficient ones.
  • Plus/minus at 2 is noisy. Don't trade for it.
  • Saves at 0.6 mean a busy goalie scores even in a loss: 30 saves with 3 against is still 9.

Head-to-head categories

Yahoo default: G, A, +/-, PPP, SOG, HIT; goalies W, GAA, SV%, SO. Each category is its own win or loss.

  • Balance wins. Hits make a fourth-line grinder worth a roster spot.
  • Late in a week you're winning, bench a shaky goalie to protect GAA and save percentage.
  • In ESPN's 'most categories' version you can punt one or two. In 'each category' leagues you can't.

Rotisserie

Season-long rank in every category; Yahoo's default swaps hits for blocks.

  • No weak categories: last place in one costs as much as a whole bad stretch.
  • Use every game your league allows at each position.

Banger leagues

Custom: adds hits, blocks and often penalty minutes.

  • Physical depth forwards and defensemen are starters here, not bench filler.
  • Check the hits and blocks leaders, not just points.

Every league can change these settings, so check yours. Defaults from ESPN's and Yahoo's own pages (fall 2026).
