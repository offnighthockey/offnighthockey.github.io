How to use this

In leagues with daily lineups, a fourth game is a free extra start. The bigger edge is the light nights: with only a few games on, most of your roster sits, so a pickup from a team that plays that night fills a slot that would otherwise score nothing. Stream skaters and goalies from the teams above, and bench players whose teams only play twice.
