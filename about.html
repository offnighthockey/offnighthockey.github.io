About

Fantasy hockey from people who play it

We run an ESPN points league around day jobs, like most of you. Off-Night Hockey is the site we wanted on game night and couldn't find.

The goal

Be the first tab you open on game night. Fantasy football managers get dozens of sites; hockey managers get a handful. Everything here is free, with no account and no ads.

How it works

The numbers come from the NHL's public stats, pulled every morning, plus ESPN's public injury list and goalie calls. Code does the math. A person makes the weekly calls. Every pick shows how many games it rests on and why, so you can check our work and disagree with it.

Ground rules

Team colors, never team logos or player photos: those belong to the NHL and the clubs. Your roster and league link stay in your own browser. The site has no server and never sees them.
